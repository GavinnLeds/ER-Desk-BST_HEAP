ER Desk: one hospital, two data structures

Every arriving patient goes into the records room (a binary search tree, searched by patient ID) and the triage queue (a max-heap, ordered by severity). Watch each operation step by step.

Records room: Binary Search Tree

Rule: smaller IDs go left, larger IDs go right. Finding a file means skipping half the remaining cabinet at every step.
search / insert / delete: O(log n) balanced, O(n) worst0 records

Triage queue: Max-Heap

Rule: every parent is at least as severe as its children, so the sickest patient is always at the top. Ties go to whoever arrived first.
peek: O(1)insert / treat next: O(log n)children of i: 2i+1, 2i+20 waiting
